T – THEORY: Hashing vs encryption

Hashing is generally one-way and produces a digest for integrity/password verification. Encryption is reversible with the correct key and protects confidentiality. Password hashes are not decrypted; guesses are tested. Use salted Argon2id/bcrypt/scrypt/PBKDF2 rather than plain MD5/SHA-256 for passwords.

T – THEORY: TCP vs UDP

TCP is connection-oriented and reliable/ordered (e.g. HTTPS, SSH). UDP sends datagrams without guaranteeing delivery/order (e.g. DNS queries, streaming or games).
